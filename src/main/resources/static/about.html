Why we started

Built by people who love this island

Lodge began with a simple frustration: it was hard to find Sri Lankan villas online that actually looked like their photos, had real availability, and could be booked without a dozen back-and-forth messages. So we built a small, honest booking platform focused on one country and done properly.

Every villa on Lodge is checked before it goes live - facilities, photos and pricing are kept up to date, and our support team is a phone call away if a stay needs a helping hand.
